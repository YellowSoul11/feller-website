## Platform

web

## Stack
React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS (user's stated preference for a new project). GSAP only where scroll sequencing adds clear value; CSS otherwise. Greenfield folder (no existing app). Deploy target: undecided; current live site runs on Lovable and must not be replaced without approval.

## Users
Owners and managers of small-to-medium businesses in Israel (confirmed in interview, 2026-11-06) who need a professional, distinctive web presence that brings inquiries — and who would benefit from marketing, automation and AI connected to that site. They evaluate a freelancer/studio by the work shown, by whether the person behind it seems to understand their business, and by how easy it is to start a conversation (WhatsApp is a primary channel).

## Product Purpose
Feller Marketing is Liem's independent digital practice. The website must explain what Liem does, show real work, build personal trust, and generate qualified inquiries. Success = visitors understand the core offer within the first screen and contact Liem via the form drawer or WhatsApp.

## Positioning
"In the age of AI, differentiation starts with human understanding." Liem combines website design and development with digital marketing, automation and AI — but every project starts with understanding the business, its goals and customers. Personal involvement plus technical range: not a generic agency, not an AI SaaS.

## Operating Context

## Product Principles
1. Show the real work before talking about yourself; label it honestly.
2. Understanding before execution — the copy and structure should demonstrate it, not claim it.
3. One clear next step on every screen: talk to Liem.
4. Websites are the centre; everything else is presented as what connects to the site.
5. Personal over corporate: one person's voice and face.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
